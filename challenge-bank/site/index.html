Build your own paper →

Pick any questions, from any subject, and assemble them into a printable paper laid out like a real exam — cover page, instructions, questions numbered with their marks in the margin, writing space for each answer, and a markscheme on its own pages.

  1. Tick Paper next to a question — here in the search results, or on a subject page.
  2. A bar appears at the bottom of the screen showing how many you have picked. It follows you from page to page, so you can build a paper across subjects.
  3. Press Build paper, tick Include the markscheme if you want the answers, then Print / Save as PDF.

How to use this bank

  • Attempt the question in writing first — timed, closed book, under exam conditions for that paper.
  • Only then open Reveal the answer. Compare method, not just the final value.
  • Read Markscheme notes for what earns partial credit and what the common errors are.
  • To work away from the screen, tick Paper on the questions you want and print them from the paper builder; or print a whole subject at once.
  • Read Why this question is hard last; it names the trap the question is built around.
