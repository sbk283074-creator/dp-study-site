IB Challenge Bank

A small, deliberately hard set of original exam-style questions for the class of 2028 session: Maths AA HL, Physics HL, Computer Science HL and Business Management SL.

Every question is written from scratch against a specific syllabus bullet. Nothing is a past-paper question with the numbers changed, and nothing is a one-step recall item. Each question carries a stated challenge lever — the thing that makes it hard — and shows its markscheme, its provenance and how it was verified.

How to use this bank

  • Attempt the question in writing first — timed, closed book, under exam conditions for that paper.
  • Only then open Reveal the answer. Compare method, not just the final value.
  • Read Markscheme notes for what earns partial credit and what the common errors are.
  • Read Why this question is hard last; it names the trap the question is built around.
