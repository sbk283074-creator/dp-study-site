A loop that is linear only while the data keeps moving

A performance claim carries an unstated assumption about the input: the nested loop is fast only when consecutive readings differ by more than the tolerance, so it degenerates to quadratic behaviour on exactly the steady data the application exists to detect.

A 12-bit temperature log and the arithmetic that breaks it

The word length is fixed and small enough that representable range, scaled range and accumulator overflow all interact; the student must recognise that the overflow point depends on the number of readings rather than on the sensor, and then explain why one conversion factor is exact and a superficially similar one is not.

An irrigation gateway choosing how to share its processor

Two scheduling policies must be compared not by reputation but by computed average waiting time, and the intuitively fairer policy turns out to be worse on that measure here; the student then has to justify the choice on the basis of worst-case behaviour instead, which requires reading the numbers in a way that the average conceals.
