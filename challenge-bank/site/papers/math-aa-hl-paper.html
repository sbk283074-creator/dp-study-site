1. 5.18 First order differential equations — coupled mixing model

Let $x(t)$ and $y(t)$ be the masses, in kg, of dissolved salt in tanks $A$ and $B$ after $t$ minutes.

  1. [2 marks](a)

    Show that $y$ satisfies $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}+\dfrac{y}{30}=1-e^{-t/20}$.

    (Show that)
  4. [5 marks](d)

    Hence find $y(t)$, given that tank $B$ initially contains pure water.

    (Hence)
  5. [3 marks](e)

    Show that the mass of salt in tank $B$ never exceeds $30$ kg.

    (Show that)

2. Random walks, difference equations and expected duration

[20 marks]MATH-P3-002

A counter moves on the integers $0,1,2,\ldots,N$. At each step it moves up by $1$ with probability $p$ and down by $1$ with probability $q=1-p$, independently of all earlier steps. The game stops when the counter first reaches $0$ or $N$. Let $u_k$ denote the probability that the counter reaches $N$ before $0$, given that it starts at $k$.

  1. [3 marks](a)

    Explain why $u_k=p\,u_{k+1}+q\,u_{k-1}$ for $1\le k\le N-1$, and state the values of $u_0$ and $u_N$.

    (Explain)
  2. [4 marks](b)

    Show that when $p\ne q$ the expression $u_k=\dfrac{\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)^{k}-1}{\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)^{N}-1}$ satisfies the recurrence in (a) together with the two boundary conditions.

    (Show that)
  3. [4 marks](c)

    For the case $p=q=\tfrac12$, use mathematical induction to prove that $u_k=\dfrac{k}{N}$ for every integer $k$ with $0\le k\le N$.

    (Prove)
  4. [3 marks](d)

    Let $D$ be the number of steps taken before the game stops, and let $d_k=\mathrm{E}(D\mid\text{start at }k)$. In the symmetric case $p=q=\tfrac12$, show that $d_k=1+\tfrac12 d_{k+1}+\tfrac12 d_{k-1}$, and verify that $d_k=k(N-k)$ satisfies it together with $d_0=d_N=0$.

    (Show that)
  5. [3 marks](e)

    Take $N=10$, $k=5$ and $p=0.48$. Calculate $u_5$ to three significant figures.

    (Calculate)
  6. [3 marks](f)

    Determine the expected number of steps in the symmetric game with $N=10$ and $k=5$, and comment on how the answer to (e) changes the picture even though the game looks almost fair.

    (Determine)
